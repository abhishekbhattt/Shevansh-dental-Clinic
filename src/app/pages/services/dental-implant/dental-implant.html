Why Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the most advanced solution for replacing missing teeth. They restore natural function, improve aesthetics, and provide long-term durability. Implants help maintain jawbone health and give you the confidence of a complete smile.

Procedure Steps

  • Consultation: Dentist evaluates oral health and suitability for implants.

  • Imaging & Planning: X-rays or 3D scans to plan precise implant placement.

  • Surgical Placement: Titanium implant is placed into the jawbone.

  • Healing Period: Bone integrates with the implant (osseointegration).

  • Abutment & Crown: A custom crown is attached for a natural look and feel.
