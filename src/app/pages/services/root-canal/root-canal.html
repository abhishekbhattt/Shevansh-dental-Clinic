Why Root Canal Treatment?

A root canal saves severely decayed or infected teeth by removing damaged pulp, cleaning the canals, and sealing them. This treatment relieves pain, prevents further infection, and preserves your natural tooth.

Procedure Steps

  • Diagnosis: X-rays and examination to confirm infection.

  • Anesthesia: Local anesthesia ensures a painless procedure.

  • Pulp Removal: Infected pulp is carefully removed from the canals.

  • Cleaning & Shaping: Canals are disinfected and shaped for filling.

  • Sealing: Canals are sealed with biocompatible material to prevent reinfection.

  • Restoration: A crown or filling restores strength and function.
