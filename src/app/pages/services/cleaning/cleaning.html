Why Teeth Cleaning?

Professional teeth cleaning prevents gum disease, cavities, and bad breath. By removing plaque and tartar, we help you maintain long‑term oral health and a confident smile.

Procedure Steps

  • Initial Examination: Dentist checks teeth and gums to identify issues.

  • Scaling: Removes plaque and tartar using ultrasonic instruments.

  • Polishing: Smoothens tooth surfaces for a shiny finish.

  • Fluoride Treatment: Strengthens enamel and prevents decay.

  • Final Check: Dentist reviews results and shares hygiene tips.
